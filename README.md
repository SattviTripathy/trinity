# ✒ Trinity

A tiny, offline log for your fountain-pen combinations — **pen · ink · paper** — with a quality rating and a pairing recommender.

**Live:** https://sattvitripathy.github.io/trinity/

## What it does
- Log any **pen + ink + paper** combination with a **Quality** rating (Poor → Excellent).
- Mark whether it's your **default go-to pairing** (checked by default) or an experiment.
- Add free-text **comments** (feathering, dry time, sheen, feel…).
- **Pairing recommender:** pick a pen and/or ink you've logged and Trinity ranks the papers by your own average quality — so you know what to reach for.
- Search, filter (by quality / default vs experimental), sort any column, and **export to CSV**.
- Light/dark theme.

## Privacy
Everything lives in your browser's `localStorage`. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Tech
A single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies.
