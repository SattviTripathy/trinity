FAQ
What is Trinity?
A log for your fountain-pen combinations. Its heart is the trinity of pen, ink and paper. Record each combination, rate the experience and add notes, and over time it becomes your personal reference for what writes well together.
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account or server.
How do I back up or move my data?
Open the ⋯ menu on the log. Backup saves a file with all your entries, photos and inventory. Import loads it back on another device. Export CSV gives a spreadsheet copy of the log.
How do I install it on my phone?
Trinity is a PWA, so it installs straight from the browser and then opens in its own window and works offline.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Open sattvitripathy.github.io/trinity in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. Tap Install to confirm. The nib icon appears on your home screen.

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the site in Safari (this only works in Safari, not Chrome on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an upward arrow) in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. The nib icon appears on your home screen.
My pen or ink is from a small maker. Will it work?
Just type the full name. The suggestions only save keystrokes for common brands. Anything you type is accepted and remembered for next time.
How do I add a photo?
In the add sheet, tap Add photo. On a phone this can open the camera. The image is shrunk on your device before it is saved, so it stays small.
What is the Inventory tab?
A list of the pens, inks, notebooks and papers you own. Whenever you log a combination, any new pen, ink or paper is added here automatically. Your items feed the autofill suggestions across the app, and anything you own but have not paired yet is tagged untried, so it doubles as a to-try list. New papers land under Papers, since the log does not split notebooks from loose paper.
For the geeks
How does the pairing recommender work?
You decide which corners you have and which you want Trinity to find. Then:
  1. It keeps only the log entries that match every corner you marked I have it and filled in.
  2. It groups those entries by whatever you marked Find it, whether that is one corner (a single paper, ink or pen) or two (a pairing).
  3. Each experience becomes a number: Poor 1, Fair 2, Good 3, Great 4, Excellent 5, and it averages them per group.
  4. It ranks the groups by that average, highest first. Ties go to the group with more entries, since that is stronger evidence.
  5. The pill is the average rounded to the nearest word, and the line shows the exact average and the entry count.
Mark nothing as had and all three as found to see your best combinations overall. Nibs are pooled, since they belong to the pen. The more you log, the sharper it gets.
Where is my data stored?
Entries live in localStorage under trinity.entries.v1 as a JSON array, with photos held as small compressed data inside each entry. Profile and theme are stored the same way. Backup exports that JSON, and Import replaces the current entries with it.
How do offline and install work?
A service worker caches the app on first load, so Trinity opens with no connection. A new version is fetched in the background and applies on your next visit.
