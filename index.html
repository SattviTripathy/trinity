FAQ

What is Trinity?
A simple log for your fountain-pen combinations. Record the pen, nib, ink, and paper, rate the experience, and add notes. Over time it becomes your personal reference for what writes well together.
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account or server.
How do I back up or move my data?
Use Backup to save a small file with all your entries. Use Import on another device or browser to load it back in. Export CSV gives you a spreadsheet-friendly copy.
Can I install it like an app?
Yes. Trinity is a PWA. Use your browser's Install app or Add to Home Screen option. After that it opens in its own window and works offline.
My pen or ink is from a small maker and is not in the list. What do I do?
Just type the full name. The suggestions are only there to save keystrokes for common brands. Any text you type is accepted and remembered for next time.
How do I add an unusual nib?
Pick Other at the bottom of the nib list and a box appears where you can describe it, for example an architect grind or a custom cursive italic.
For the geeks how it works under the hood
How does the pairing recommender work?
It turns your own ratings into a ranking. Given a pen and/or ink:
  1. It filters your log to entries that match. Either field can be left blank, which acts as a wildcard, so you can ask "best paper for this ink, any pen".
  2. It groups the matches by paper.
  3. Each experience becomes a number: Poor 1, Fair 2, Good 3, Great 4, Excellent 5. It averages those per paper.
  4. It ranks papers by that average, highest first. When two papers tie, the one with more entries wins, since more entries means stronger evidence.
  5. The pill you see is the average rounded to the nearest word, and the line shows the exact average and how many entries back it.
The nib is recorded but not part of the key, so all nibs for that pen and ink are pooled together. It only ever uses your data, so the more you log, the sharper it gets.
Where and how is my data stored?
Entries live in your browser's localStorage under the key trinity.entries.v1, as a JSON array. Your profile and theme are stored the same way. Backup exports that JSON to a file, and Import reads it back and replaces the current entries.
How does offline and install work?
A service worker caches the app files on first load, so Trinity opens even with no connection. When a new version is deployed, the worker fetches it in the background and it applies on your next visit.
